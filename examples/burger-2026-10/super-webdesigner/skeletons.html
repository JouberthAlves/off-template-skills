  • Edges: square, 0 to 2 px. Cut steel and a knife cut are straight.
  • Depth: only the photo's own. No shadows; the light lives in the food.

Visual language

  • Edges: organic, from the burger's own cut-out silhouette. Everything else is square.
  • Depth: one soft contact shadow under the cut-out, where the light falls.
  • Lines: hand-drawn leader lines, as on a butcher's chart. Nothing else is outlined.
  • Surface: the bun's crumb colour, flat. No paper texture.
  • Type voice: plain and proud: the butcher naming the cuts.
  • Motion feel: calm. The lines draw themselves in once.

Wildcard, logo, motion

  • Wildcard: tap or hover a label and its layer lifts 6 px from the half.
  • Logo: HALFWAY in slab, with a dashed cut line through the middle. Favicon: a half circle.
  • Motion (calm): the leader lines draw in one after another (600 ms each); the sticker settles at a 6° tilt. Hover on labels.
