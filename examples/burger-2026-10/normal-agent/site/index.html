Our story

A food truck, a flat-top and a stubborn idea.

Ember & Bun started in 2014 as a single truck parked outside a brewery. We had one griddle, one burger and one rule: smash it hard, season it right and serve it fast.

Ten years later we still grind our beef every morning from a family ranch two hours north, buns come warm from the bakery down the street, and the fries are cut by hand before we open the doors.
