# Brief: Steno (fictional AI meeting-notes app)

**Draft:** the agent answered the 4 questions itself (a mistake: the skill
now says to ask). They are being sent to the owner; the answers replace
these.

**The product:** joins your calls, writes the notes, lists who agreed to do
what. Sold to small teams; free tier, then per seat.

## The 4 questions

1. **Real-world object:** a court stenographer's machine and its paper tape:
   every word, in order, nothing lost. *Palette:* tape cream, typewriter
   ink, the green "recording" lamp.
2. **Feeling in the first 3 seconds:** relief: "I can stop typing during
   the meeting".
3. **Two unexpected influences:** court stenography × film subtitles
   (captions appearing word by word).
4. **Must never look like:** an AI startup page (dark, purple glow, sparkle
   icons, robot mascots) or a corporate SaaS page with stock photos of
   people high-fiving.

**Name:** Steno.

## Research (short)

- **Keep:** the promise in one line, "works with Zoom / Meet / Teams", a real
  example of the notes it writes, pricing, privacy.
- **Avoid:** dark mode with purple glow, sparkle icons, bento feature grids,
  "10M meetings recorded" counters. Common across Fireflies, Otter-style and
  most generated AI pages.
- **Outlier:** Granola: warm cream, hand-drawn logo, product screenshot as
  the hero.

Source: the same search as `design/research.md`, plus
[Granola design breakdown, shadcn.io](https://www.shadcn.io/design/granola).

## The 3 directions

- **A, best of the niche:** "Reporter's Notebook": the product-as-hero
  restraint of the outlier, on a yellow legal pad.
- **B, break the convention:** "Live Captions": everyone is dark; B is bright
  white with black subtitle bars, the hero is a call with captions burning in.
- **C, outside reference:** "Steno Tape": the stenotype paper tape running
  down the page, the notes printing on it.
