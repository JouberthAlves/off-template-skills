Our story

A food truck, a flat-top, and one very stubborn recipe.

Ember & Bun started in 2014 as a single truck parked outside the old rail yard. We had one griddle, one burger, and a line that wrapped around the block by noon.

Ten years later we still grind our beef in-house every morning, still smash every patty by hand, and still make our sauce from the same handwritten card taped above the line.

  • Beef from two family farms within 60 miles
  • Fries cut, soaked and double-fried in-house
  • Compostable packaging since 2019
