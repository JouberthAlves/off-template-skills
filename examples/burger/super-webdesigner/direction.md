# Chosen direction (2026-10-20)

**Base:** direction A, "Butcher Paper Counter": the paper order ticket and
the butcher's wrap, set in the restraint of the best burger brands (paper and
one ink).

**Changes the person asked for:** none.

**First screen:** desktop, left 45% on cream paper: logo, nav, the title in
three huge condensed uppercase lines, one line of text, "Order pickup" (filled)
and "See the menu" (outlined). Right 55%: the burger half wrapped in kraft
paper on a steel counter, bleeding off the top, right and bottom edges. A thin
printed price strip closes the screen. Phone: photo on top, stamp on its
corner, title, stacked buttons, price strip.

**Wildcard element:** the rubber stamp "No. 27" in rust ink, crooked, over the
lower-left corner of the photo. It presses in once on load. When someone
places an order, their ticket gets the same stamp with their number.

**Palette:** `#F3EBDD` paper · `#CDAA7D` kraft (rules, surfaces) · `#1C1B19`
marker (text, buttons) · `#9B3B24` stamp ink (the only accent: stamp, prices
of the day, focus). All four sampled from the brief's objects.

**Fonts:** Big Shoulders Display 800-900 (titles, logo, prices) · Work Sans
400-600 (text).

**Imagery:** generated photos from the mockups (fictional brand): the wrapped
burger for the hero, the burger on the steel counter by the window for "The
counter". Daylight, no smoke, no flames.

**Motion:** the stamp press on load; the price list lines print in on scroll.
Both off with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Keep list from the research:** Order button on the first screen, menu with
prices in HTML, address, hours and "open now", real food photos.

**Avoid list for this project:** the 10 general anti-patterns + dark
background with red/yellow, grunge/brick/chalkboard, smoke and flames, script
"since", emoji as menu icons, invented reviews and star ratings.

**Dropped from the skeleton:** the Instagram section. A fictional place has no
real posts, and inventing social proof is on the avoid list.

**Language:** English.

**Licenses:** fonts from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License). Photos
generated in Magnific by the repo owner.

Reference mockup: `mockup-a-butcher-paper.webp`.
