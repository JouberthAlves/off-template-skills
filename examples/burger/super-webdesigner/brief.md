# Brief: Order 27 (fictional)

**Deviation:** the agent answered the 4 questions itself instead of asking
the person. The skill now forbids that; this file is kept as it happened.

**The business:** a small smash-burger counter, 8 stools and a pickup window,
in a busy downtown block. Mostly takeaway at lunch and late at night. Ticket
around $14. Paid for by its owner-cook, not a chain.

## The 4 questions

1. **Real-world place or object:** the paper order ticket clipped to the
   kitchen rail, with a number written in marker, waiting for "order up".
   *The palette comes from it:* ticket paper, marker black, grill steel.
2. **Feeling in the first 3 seconds:** hungry right now. (Not "cozy", not
   "premium": this is a lunch break.)
3. **Two unexpected influences:** railway timetable boards (strict grid,
   times, numbers) × butcher-shop paper wrap (kraft, stamps, one ink color).
4. **Must never look like:** a fast-food chain (red and yellow, a mascot) or a
   craft-beer bar (black chalkboard, grunge, script "since 19xx").

**Name:** "Order 27". The number on the ticket is the brand.

## Rules applied

- No hex from memory: the colors were sampled from what the 4 answers describe
  (ticket paper, marker, steel, stamp ink).
- Style log: empty (first project), nothing to avoid repeating yet.
