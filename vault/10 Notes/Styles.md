# Styles

Hub of styles per niche: what each kind of business usually has on its site
and what the competitors look like. Each niche is a `Styles of <niche>` note
linked here, written by the [[Research real sites]] step.

Use before a site in a niche: the niche note says **what the client expects
to find** (keep) and **what everyone already looks like** (avoid). The form
still comes from the [[Design brief]]: the niche's look is exactly what not to
copy.

## New niche

1. Collect the niche's sites (web search, a browser, or the person's list).
2. Create `10 Notes/Styles of <niche>.md` with the sections below.
3. Add a row to the table above.
