Direction B · break the convention

Swatch Book

Every AI page is dark. This one is built from big flat color chips, like a printer's swatch book, each with its code. From the research: dark mode, glass and glow are the niche's default; color fields are the opposite.

Direction C · outside reference

Gallery Wall

A museum room: the two sites hang as framed works on a white wall, with small wall labels. The slider is the big frame in the middle. From the research: nobody in the niche shows work like art; the calm wall label is the opposite of a feature grid.
