# Brief: the Super-Webdesigner showcase page

The page that sells the project: a drag slider comparing "Other agents" with
"Super-Webdesigner", the 10 tells, the flow, install. First version failed
the flow (built straight to code, no logo, no favicon, colors the owner
disliked). This is the redo, through the flow.

**Who it's for:** developers and designers who use coding agents and are
tired of every page looking the same. They land from GitHub or a post, look
for 10 seconds, and either drag the slider or leave.

## The 4 questions (answered by the owner, 2026-10-20)

1. **Real-world object:** the print proof marked up in red pencil by the art
   director. *Palette:* proof white, press black, red pencil.
2. **Feeling in the first 3 seconds:** relief: "finally, someone solved
   this".
3. **Two unexpected influences:** a print shop's press proof × a museum wall
   label.
4. **Must never look like:** a generic docs site, a generic AI site, or
   anything weakly designed. The bar: a visitor thinks "damn, this site is
   great".

**Name:** Super-Webdesigner (renamed: "Superdesign" already exists).

**Logo and favicon:** required in every direction.

## Slider requirements (from the owner)

- Labels: "Other agents" and "Super-Webdesigner".
- Each label lives inside its own side and disappears with it: with the line
  all the way left, "Other agents" is gone.
- The drag has to feel good: follows the pointer 1:1, eases when you click a
  spot, a short hint sweep on first view, keyboard arrows.
