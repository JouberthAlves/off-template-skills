# Research: AI tool and design-tool landing pages

Same network limits as the burger example (corporate proxy): web search and
published design breakdowns, no third-party screenshots kept.

## Keep: what visitors expect

- A one-line promise and the install command on the first screen.
- A live demo above the fold (here: the slider).
- A link to the GitHub repo.
- Proof that is real: the two sites are in the repo.

## Avoid: the niche's sameness

The 2026 roundups list the same moves for AI and dev-tool pages: dark mode,
glassmorphism and frosted cards, bento grids, oversized display type and
"complex scroll animations baked in" by generators. Templates for "editorial
AI design" lean on a large serif on near-black for "quiet luxury". All of it
is the look this project argues against, so it's the avoid list:

- Dark background with glow or gradient.
- Frosted glass cards, bento grid of features.
- Grid or dot backgrounds.
- Serif-on-black "luxury" heroes.
- A terminal window as the hero image.

## Premium vs. generic

The pages people remember make the product the hero and keep everything
else quiet. The outlier in AI productivity is Granola: warm cream instead of
dark mode, a hand-drawn imperfect logo on purpose.

## How the directions use it

- **A, best of the niche:** product-as-hero restraint, on proof white with one
  red pencil.
- **B, break the convention:** every AI page is dark; B is full saturated color
  fields, like a swatch book.
- **C, outside reference:** a museum: the two sites hung like artworks, with
  wall labels.
