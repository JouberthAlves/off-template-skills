# Wins

Append only. What the person praised, so it becomes a pattern instead of luck.
Each entry also goes, in one line, under "Patterns that worked" in
[[Design rules]] (or the area's rules note).

The entries below come with the vault, from the projects the off-template
skill was built on. Keep them, or delete them once you have your own.

## Template

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: short title
- **Project:** ...
- **What was praised:** (the person's words, if possible)
- **Why it worked:** ...
- **Pattern:** (one line, reusable in other projects)
```

## 2026-09-23: a slider that shows the view at each hour
- **Project:** an apartment launch facing a park
- **What was praised:** "this is the thing people will play with"
- **Why it worked:** the product's main argument (the view) became something the visitor moves in the hero, instead of a photo with a caption.
- **Pattern:** find the product's number-one argument and let the visitor handle it on the first screen.

## 2026-09-28: the identity came from the restaurant's own objects
- **Project:** a home-style restaurant
- **What was praised:** "it looks like the place"
- **Why it worked:** palette, type and texture came from the paper order pad, the enamel plates and the counter; every generated photo shared one base scene.
- **Pattern:** take the identity from the business's physical objects and lock one base scene for every photo.

## 2026-10-01: gray skeleton plus a colored mockup per direction
- **Project:** a barbershop
- **What was praised:** "now I can choose"
- **Why it worked:** the skeleton showed composition, the mockup showed the feel; together they made the choice easy and the build had no surprises.
- **Pattern:** show composition (gray) and feel (one colored mockup) for each direction before asking for the choice.

## 2026-10-02: the look came from the client's own posts
- **Project:** a personal trainer with a strong Instagram
- **What was praised:** "it's me"
- **Why it worked:** palette, letterforms and tone were sampled from the client's posts, and the most recognizable element went to the first screen.
- **Pattern:** a client with their own identity in posts: sample it, don't invent a new one.

## 2026-10-26: a beam of light that lights the page
- **Project:** the off-template showcase site
- **What was praised:** the red light column in the hero
- **Why it worked:** a reference the person loved (a light pillar effect) was rebuilt in an own shader, in the brand's red, and it lights the headline and the print as it passes instead of sitting behind them.
- **Pattern:** an animated background from a reference: rebuild it in the brand colour and make it act on the page's elements; lower intensity on phones.
