# Halfway

**Style:** Fisheye Session

A burger cut in half, shot like a frame of a skate video through a fisheye
lens in front of the truck at night. The first 3 seconds feel loud and a
little crooked, like a friend saying "you have to try this". The collision:
skate video × butcher shop (white butcher paper wrap, plain proud copy). The
wildcard: the round photo drifts with the pointer like a handheld camera.

**Palette:** `#3c2828` char · `#7e321b` edge char · `#e9ab68` cheese (only the
order button) · `#dcbc9d` bun crumb · **Fonts:** Bowlby One / Karla

**Direction file:** `examples/burger-2026-10/off-template/direction.md` in the
off-template repo.

Comes with the vault as an example. A new burger site reading this note must
not repeat this style: another reference, another palette, other faces.
