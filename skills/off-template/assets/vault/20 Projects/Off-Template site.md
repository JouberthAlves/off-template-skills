# Off-Template site

**Style:** Darkroom Develop

The editor at night meets a photographer's darkroom: a page develops in a
tray under a red safelight, from gray skeleton to colored mockup to finished
page, which is the skill's own flow. A red beam of light comes down from above
and lights the letters it touches. The wildcard: an agent window with three
prompts; picking one rebuilds the rest of the page in that look, growing from
the click as a circle with a liquid rim.

**Palette:** `#141818` night · `#1c2120` tray · `#E5231B` safelight red (the
only accent) · `#f4f4f4` headline · `#acb0bc` text · **Fonts:** Familjen
Grotesk / Commit Mono

**Direction file:** `design/direction.md` in the off-template repo. Live at
https://off-template-skills.vercel.app

Comes with the vault as an example.
