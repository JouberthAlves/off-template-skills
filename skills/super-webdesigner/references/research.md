# Research real sites in the niche

Before drawing directions, look at what already exists. The research tells you
two different things, and mixing them up is the trap:

- what visitors **expect to find** (keep it: missing it costs trust);
- what **everyone in the niche looks like** (avoid it: copying it makes you
  generic, just generic for the niche).

## 1. Collect 8 to 15 sites

Sources, in this order: links the person sent; web search for the niche plus
the city or country ("burger restaurant website", "best burger restaurant
websites", award galleries like Awwwards or Land-book filtered by the niche);
the competitors the person names. Mix ordinary local sites with the best ones
you can find: you need both the norm and the ceiling.

Open each one in a browser you can drive and screenshot the first screen at
desktop and 390 px. Screenshots stay local: never commit third-party
screenshots to a public repo.

## 2. Note the same things for every site

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Section order | Hero → menu → about → ... |
| Hero layout | Centered text over photo, split, full-bleed video, product cutout... |
| Palette | Main colors in words and hex (sampled from the screenshot) |
| Fonts | Family or style (condensed sans, script, slab, mono) |
| Imagery | Product photo, people, illustration, stock, user photos |
| Motion | None, scroll reveals, video, hover effects |
| Main CTA | Text and where it sits |
| Tier | Generic, solid, premium, outlier |

## 3. Turn it into two lists

**Keep (what visitors expect).** What 70% or more of the sites have and a
visitor would miss: for a burger place, menu with prices, address and map,
hours, order/delivery button, photos of the actual food.

**Avoid (the niche's sameness).** The look most of them share: e.g. black
background with red and yellow, grunge textures, a script font for "since
19xx", a dark hero photo with a burger and smoke. This becomes a niche-specific
list of anti-patterns for this project, on top of the general ones.

Also write down: **what separates premium from generic** (usually photography
quality, restraint in color, real typography, one strong idea) and **the
outliers** (who breaks the rules and still works, and how).

## 4. How each direction uses the research

- **A, best of the niche:** at the level of the premium sites, keeping
  everything in the keep list and none of the avoid list.
- **B, break the convention:** take the strongest item of the avoid list and
  do the opposite on purpose.
- **C, outside reference:** bring in a world from outside the niche (the
  brief's two unexpected influences) while keeping the keep list.

Each skeleton says which finding it came from ("B: every site is black and red,
so B is daylight cream with one ketchup red").

## 5. Save it

Vault: `10 Notes/Styles of <niche>.md`, linked from `Styles.md`, so the next
project in the same niche starts from it. No vault: `design/research.md` in
the project.

## When sites won't open

Some sites will fail (blocked network, certificate errors). List which ones
and keep going with the rest. If fewer than 8 opened, say so and ask the
person for links at STOP 1. Don't fill the gap with guesses or with
second-hand articles presented as if you had seen the sites.

## Rules

- Extract patterns, never copy images, copy or logos from another site.
- No search or browser available at all: ask the person for 5+ links, or for
  screenshots, and analyze those.
